Graphing Calculator

How to use

  • Type an equation such as x^2, 2x+1 or sin(x) and the graph is drawn instantly. Your equation is shown below in standard math notation (x²).
  • Drag the graph to move it. Use the mouse wheel or pinch with two fingers to zoom.
  • Supported: + − * / ^ ( ), sqrt, abs, sin, cos, tan, asin, acos, atan, ln, log, exp, floor, ceil, pi, e. The buttons sin⁻¹, cos⁻¹ and tan⁻¹ insert asin, acos and atan, the inverse trigonometric functions. ln is the natural logarithm and log is base 10.
  • Equations with both x and y also work, such as x^2+y^2=1 or y^2=x.
  • Under any y = f(x) equation, click Details of graph to see intercepts, maximum and minimum points, inflection points, asymptotes, end behavior and derivatives.
  • You can leave out the multiplication sign (2x works). Up to 4 equations can be plotted at once.
