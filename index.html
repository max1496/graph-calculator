Quick start

  • Type an equation such as x^2 or sin(x). The graph appears at once.
  • Drag to move the graph. Scroll or pinch to zoom.
  • Under the equation, tap Details of graph to see intercepts, high and low points, and asymptotes.
  • Use Copy link to share your graph, or Save image to download it.
More tips and supported features
  • Supported: + − * / ^ ( ), sqrt, abs, sin, cos, tan, asin, acos, atan, ln, log, exp, floor, ceil, pi, e. The buttons sin⁻¹, cos⁻¹ and tan⁻¹ insert asin, acos and atan, the inverse trigonometric functions. ln is the natural logarithm and log is base 10.
  • Equations with both x and y also work, such as x^2+y^2=1 or y^2=x.
  • Under any y = f(x) equation, click Details of graph to see intercepts, maximum and minimum points, inflection points, asymptotes, end behavior and derivatives.
  • Piecewise functions: add a condition in braces, for example x {x<2}, x+1 {x>=2}. Open circles mark points that are not included, filled circles mark points that are, and the interval such as [2, ∞) is shown under the equation. Use one equation per piece or separate pieces with a comma.
  • Inequalities: type y>x, y<=x^2 or x>=2 to shade the solution. A dashed boundary means the line itself is not included.
  • Click the colored circle next to an equation to change its color.
  • You can leave out the multiplication sign (2x works). Up to 4 equations can be plotted at once.
