About

This is a free graphing calculator that runs in your browser. Type an equation such as x^2 or sin(x) and the graph appears at once. You can zoom, move the view, show the x-axis in units of π, view the graph full screen, and plot up to four equations together. Piecewise functions and inequalities are supported too.

Why it was made

Most graphing tools only draw the curve. This site also explains it. Under any y = f(x) equation, Details of graph lists the intercepts, maximum and minimum points, inflection points, asymptotes, end behavior and derivatives, and the learning pages explain how to find each of these by hand.

What you can do here

  • Graphing calculator for functions and equations such as x^2 + y^2 = 1
  • Pin points and draw tangent lines on a graph, and keep a history of your recent graphs (saved only in your own browser)
  • Trigonometry, conic sections, parent functions, function transformations, what is a function, precalculus (inverse functions, continuity and derivatives, polynomial degrees) and geometry transformations
  • Linear function page with slope and intercept sliders
  • Quadratic function page with vertex, discriminant and roots
  • Linear regression calculator with R² and standard deviation
  • Algebra 1 and Algebra 2 study pages that collect the tools in a sensible order
  • Polynomial from roots, Rational Root Theorem, Descartes' Rule of Signs and Intermediate Value Theorem calculators

How the results are calculated

Everything is computed in your browser. The equations and data you type are not sent to a server. Graphs are drawn by evaluating your equation at many points. The extrema, intercepts, inflection points and asymptotes in Details of graph are found numerically by searching x from −100 to 100, so they are close approximations rather than exact symbolic answers. Derivatives are produced by the open-source math.js library, which is loaded from a public content delivery network the first time you open Details of graph.

Limits

  • By default features are searched for x from −100 to 100. You can change this range at the bottom of Details of graph, up to ±10000. Features outside the range are not found.
  • The analysis covers equations in the form y = f(x). Equations that mix x and y are drawn but not analyzed.
  • Results can be wrong for unusual functions. Please check important work by hand.
