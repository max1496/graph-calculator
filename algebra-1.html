Algebra 1

In short: Algebra 1 in order: from slope to parabolas. Short steps, one idea each.

Algebra 1 turns numbers into relationships: when this changes, how does that change? Equations, graphs and tables all tell the same story.

The big ideas

Slope = rise ÷ run. It is the rate of change.
Linear graphs are straight lines: y = mx + b. Quadratic graphs are U-shaped: y = ax² + bx + c.
Solving an equation means finding where graphs meet (or cross the x-axis).

Study tips

  • Do one idea at a time. If it feels long, stop at the end of a section and come back.
  • Solve by hand first, then graph it to see if it makes sense.
  • Tap the search button at the bottom left to find a word or a tool.
  • Keep a mistake notebook. Reviewing misses is the fastest way to improve.

Books to study from

A textbook gives you far more practice problems than any website. These are good choices for Algebra 1.

  • Glencoe Algebra 1 (McGraw-Hill). Well explained, with many types of problems, from basic practice to challenge questions. A very common school textbook in the United States.
  • Big Ideas Math Algebra 1. Clear worked examples and good real-life word problems. Used in many school districts.
  • Art of Problem Solving: Introduction to Algebra. Much harder than a school textbook. Best for students who want to be challenged and enjoy puzzles.
  • Khan Academy Algebra 1 (free, online). Short videos and practice. Good for filling gaps next to a textbook.

Editions change often, so check that the book matches your course or teacher.
