Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This policy explains what information this website collects and how it is used. By using the site you agree to this policy.

Information you enter

The equations and data points you type into the calculators are processed in your browser. They are not sent to or stored on our server. There are no accounts, and the site does not ask for your name or other personal details.

Visitor statistics

We use Vercel Web Analytics to count visits. It records anonymous information such as the pages viewed, the referring website, your country, and the type of device, browser and operating system. It is used only to understand how the site is used and to improve it. See Vercel's privacy documentation for details of how this service works.

Third-party libraries

The first time you open Details of graph, your browser downloads the open-source math.js library from the jsDelivr content delivery network. As with any file request, the network can see your IP address and browser details. We also use Google Search Console to see how the site appears in Google Search; it does not collect information about individual visitors.

Advertising

This site may show advertisements served by Google AdSense or similar services. If ads are shown, third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's previous visits to this site and other sites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this and other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or by visiting aboutads.info. Where required by law, such as in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, ads that use cookies are shown only after you give consent.

Cookies

The calculators themselves do not set cookies. Cookies may be set by advertising partners as described above, and you can block or delete them in your browser settings.

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when new features or advertising services are added. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
